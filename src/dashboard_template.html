Which NFL positions are worth paying for?

Salary-cap spending by position against team results, 2013–2025: 416 team-seasons, 32 teams. Starters are separated from bench depth using snap counts.

  • Quarterback is the clearest payoff, and it works through the passing game. It holds up against rookie-contract QBs and under six different definitions of “starter”.
  • Spend lines up with the right side of the ball. WR and OL help the offense. EDGE and CB help the defense, through pass defense. Interior DL helps against the run.
  • Tight end looks valuable until you notice it also “improves” the defense. That cannot be real, so treat the TE result as noise.
  • Cap allocation explains only about 10% of team results. Injuries, schedule and luck do most of the rest.

Beyond the model: injuries and depth

From the notebook (sections 9 to 13). Bench spending looked wasteful at first; most of it is injury cost.

per game when the starting QB misses 8 weeks. Each missed week costs about 0.64.

~1/5

of that damage is what a replacement QB one standard deviation better should recover, and about what we measure. The data cannot tell it from zero, and cannot rule out a benefit up to about two-fifths.

~0

carry-over: last year’s injured-reserve money does not predict this year’s results. It is an injury effect, not a bad roster.

How to read this

Data. Per-player cap hits (Over the Cap), snap counts, results and EPA/play from nflverse, 2013–2025. Cap share is a position’s cap divided by the team’s tracked cap, which is only part of the official cap.

Starter means the top-N players at a position by snaps (QB1, RB1, WR3, TE1, OL5, DL2, EDGE2, LB2, CB3, S2). Everyone else is bench.

Estimates come from one regression per outcome with all positions together, season fixed effects and standard errors clustered by team. The k/6 column counts how many of six starter definitions keep the result significant at 5%. EPA is per 100 plays; defense is flipped so higher is better.

Caution. These are associations, not causal effects. Ten positions across several outcomes means a few significant results are expected by chance. The full write-up, with all checks, is in analysis.ipynb.
